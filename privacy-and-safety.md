# Privacy and safety

This is a scheduling assistant prototype, not a clinical decision system.

- Do not collect symptoms for diagnosis or provide medical advice through this workflow.
- Do not commit patient names, phone numbers, appointment exports, clinic schedules that are confidential, or screenshots containing identifiable data.
- Keep Google, WhatsApp, and n8n credentials in credential stores or environment secrets.
- Restrict access to the spreadsheet and calendar to authorized staff.
- Obtain appropriate consent before sending WhatsApp messages and follow applicable messaging rules.
- Define retention and deletion policies for patient contact and appointment data.
- Add authentication, request validation, logging controls, and monitoring before public deployment.
- Use synthetic data in demos and screenshots.
